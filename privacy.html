Pin Pricks: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

The short version

Pin Pricks doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (sound levels, controls, the Bleep switch), your scores and your progress (Career, trophies, Locker balls and Crew Tapes) are saved on your own device, inside the app. The game never sends them to us, or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them, and Settings > Reset progress clears them at any time.

On Android, the phone's own backup (if it's switched on) may keep a copy of the game's progress in your own Google account, so it can come back when you reinstall the game or set up a new phone. That copy is made by your phone, kept in your Google account and managed in your phone's backup settings; the game never sends anything to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • We don't use your location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.
  • We don't show adverts or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • The game works offline and doesn't connect to the internet.

Children

Pin Pricks contains strong language and crude humour and is meant for adults (see its age rating on the App Store). We don't knowingly collect anything from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of this (for example, if adverts are added), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
