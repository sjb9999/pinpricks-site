Help and support

Gutterby Lanes is falling apart and a developer wants to flatten it. Bowl against three rival alleys to win the money to save it, with a crew of ten foul-mouthed pins heckling you from the telly.

Questions and problems

Stuck, found a bug, or just want to complain about Billy? Email fernhollowgame@gmail.com and put "Pin Pricks" in the subject. Telling us your phone model helps.

Common questions

Can I turn the swearing off? Yes. Open Settings and turn on the Swear Jar: every swear is bleeped and shown as #@!%.

The game feels slow on my phone. Open Settings and pick Low under Looks for the smoothest play.

How do I start my Career again? Settings, then Reset progress at the bottom. This can't be undone.
